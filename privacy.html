2. Inferences and Calculated Data

Based on information you provide, we generate the following derived metrics:

2.1 Body Composition Estimates

  • Body Fat Percentage (BFP)
  • Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM)
  • Visceral Fat Level (VFL)
  • Waist Circumference (WC) estimate
  • Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR)
  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)
  • Body Mass Index (BMI)

2.2 Health and Wellness Scores

  • Health Score (0-100): Comprehensive health indicator
  • Biological Age: Estimated biological age based on lifestyle factors
  • Power Score: Composite score including health score and engagement metrics

2.3 Personalized Targets

  • Daily calorie targets
  • Protein intake recommendations
  • Hydration targets
  • Step count goals
  • Sleep duration recommendations

2.4 Profile Classifications

  • S-L-M Profile: Body type (S), Lifestyle patterns (L), Medical/medication status (M)
  • Risk classification based on WHtR and other metrics
  • Behavioral pattern analysis

6. Sensitive Health Data (Special Category Data)

6.1 What is Sensitive Data?

Under various privacy laws, certain health-related information is classified as "sensitive" or "special category" data. HAVIT collects the following sensitive data categories:

CategorySpecific DataPurpose
Health ConditionsMetabolic, cardiovascular, hormonal, musculoskeletal conditionsPersonalized safety guidance, contraindication alerts
MedicationsGLP-1 agonist usage, medication duration, side effectsM-Type classification, medication-aware recommendations
Medical HistoryAllergies, eating disorder historySafety screening, appropriate content filtering
Ethnic OriginEthnic backgroundBody composition model calibration (ethnicity affects body composition norms)

6.2 Why We Need This Data

This sensitive data is essential for HAVIT's core personalized services:

  • AI Body Composition Estimation: Ethnic background significantly affects body composition norms and risk thresholds
  • M-Type Classification: Medication status determines safe calorie targets and recommendation adjustments
  • S-L-M Profiling: Health conditions inform your personalized wellness strategy
  • Safety Alerts: Medical history helps us avoid potentially harmful recommendations

6.3 Explicit Consent Requirement

IMPORTANT: We collect sensitive health data ONLY after obtaining your explicit, informed consent.

6.3.1 How We Obtain Consent

Your consent for sensitive health data is obtained through a dedicated consent screen during onboarding that:

  • Is separate from the general Terms of Service acceptance
  • Clearly lists the specific sensitive data categories being collected
  • Explains the purpose for each data category
  • Requires affirmative action (tapping "Agree & Continue" button)
  • Cannot be bypassed — consent is required to access personalized features

6.3.2 Consent Records

We maintain records of your consent including:

  • Date and time consent was given
  • Version of consent form presented
  • Method of consent (in-app button tap)
  • IP address and device information at time of consent
